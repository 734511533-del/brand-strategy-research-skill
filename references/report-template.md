# Brand Strategy Research Report Template

> Note: all examples below are illustrative placeholders showing the expected granularity — replace entirely with research findings on the target brand.

## Section 1: Core Brand Identity & Summary

- **Limit**: ≤200 words
- **Content**: Brand identity, market positioning, key brand assets, and core challenges
- **Goal**: Give the reader a precise snapshot of what this brand is and what it faces

## Section 2: Brand DNA & Founding Philosophy

Analyze:
- **Brand origin**: What problem or value did the brand set out to address?
- **Founder imprint**: How did the founder's personal philosophy shape the brand's early personality?
- **Initial positioning**: Who was the brand trying to become for whom?

## Section 3: Brand Evolution — Milestone Analysis

**Format**: Time-axis table, 3-5 phases. Most recent 2 years must be the most detailed.

**Table columns**:
| Phase (Years) | Brand Core Concept/Slogan | Key Marketing Campaigns/Tactics | Concurrent Business/Product Progress | Brand Shaping Effect |
|---------------|---------------------------|----------------------------------|--------------------------------------|----------------------|
| Phase 1: e.g., Startup Exploration | "[founding-era slogan]" | Early user community ops, founder-led content | First-gen product launch, early funding round | Laid initial niche image; accumulated seed users |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| Latest Phase: e.g., Full Ecosystem | "[current slogan]" | Festival-season brand film, cross-industry collabs, sponsorships, livestream events | Ecosystem/category expansion, flagship sales records, new market entry | Upgraded from single-category maker to ecosystem builder |

**Research sources for latest phase**:
- Ad awards: Cannes Lions, Effie Awards, One Show, 金投赏
- Marketing media: 广告门, 数英网, SocialBeta, 胖鲸智库
- Social platforms: Weibo, Xiaohongshu, Douyin trending topics
- Official: Brand press releases, investor relations announcements

## Section 4: Product Line Brand Strategy Matrix

**Format**: Comparison table, 3-4 core product lines.

**Table columns**:
| Product Line | Target Persona | Positioning/USP | Primary Channels | Signature Tactics/Case |
|--------------|----------------|-----------------|------------------|------------------------|
| e.g., Flagship line | Professionals, enthusiasts, quality-driven buyers | "[performance/craft-led USP]" | Vertical media reviews, flagship retail, launch events | Flagship-led campaigns; high-end brand collabs |
| e.g., Entry/Trendy line | Young students, trend followers | "[accessible entry-point USP]" | Short-video platforms, Xiaohongshu, creator seeding, campus events | IP collab editions; hashtag/social challenges |
| e.g., Home/Family line | New middle-class families, renovators | "[scenario-led USP]" | Category media, designer communities, experience stores | Scenario-based content; channel partner co-marketing |

## Section 5: Brand Communication System

**Three sub-sections**:

1. **Visual Identity**: Logo evolution, brand colors, typography, design language changes and their strategic rationale
2. **Tone of Voice**: What "personality" does the brand project across ads, social media, customer service? Consistency or differentiation across product lines?
3. **Content & Channel Strategy**: Owned media matrix (brand blog, podcast, founder IP), how these choices reinforce positioning

## Section 6: Brand Competitive Landscape & Perception Gap

**Three sub-sections**:

1. **Mindshare Analysis**: vs. 2-3 key competitors — what position does this brand occupy in consumer minds?
2. **Brand Sentiment**: Online buzz volume, positive/neutral/negative ratio, dominant discussion themes
3. **Brand Crisis & Response**: Major historical crises and how the brand responded

## Section 7: Conclusion — Brand Moat & Outlook

**Three sub-sections**:

1. **Brand Moat**: The single most defensible, non-replicable asset after years of development
2. **Growth Opportunities & Risks**:
   - **Opportunities**: Biggest growth levers based on current brand assets
   - **Risks**: Brand aging, positioning blur, values controversy, competitive displacement
3. **Analyst View**: Overall brand vitality assessment + the ONE strategic move the brand should make next at the brand level
